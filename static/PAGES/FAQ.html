Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is Student Hub?

Student Hub is a platform where students can access their academic and campus-related information.

2. How can I check my attendance?

Click on the Attendance option from the Student Hub dashboard.

3. How can I view my results?

Click on the Result option from the dashboard.

4. How can I register for courses?

Select the Course Registration option from the dashboard.

5. Where can I find upcoming events?

Click on Upcoming Events to view upcoming college events.
